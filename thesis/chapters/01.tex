Recent climate regulations have led to the rise of the adoption of electric vehicles. More and more people are opting for an electric vehicle for their daily commute. According to studies most of the time spend in commute is on the way to and from the office. Also most people spend a third of their time in offices, so 
it is logical to create smart charging algorithms that can utilise information from solar power generation, pricing spreads around the day and grid capacity limits in order to optimise the charging schedule.
